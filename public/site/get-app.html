How "Get the app" works

Avva is a web app, not a download from the App Store or Google Play — opening your personal link and adding it to your Home Screen gives you the same one-tap icon and full-screen experience as a regular app.

  1. Open your personal Avva link in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share icon (square with an arrow) in the toolbar.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  1. Open your personal Avva link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu (⋮) in the top right.
  3. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
